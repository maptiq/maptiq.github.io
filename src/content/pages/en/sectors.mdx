  heading: What mission-critical means in practice
  items:
  - title: Availability
    text: Targets such as 99.9% or higher, set per system and met through redundancy and automatic failover.
  - title: Recoverability
    text: Agreed recovery time and data loss limits, backed by regular, documented restore drills.
  - title: Data integrity
    text: Constraints, topology rules, history and audit trail, so you can always say who changed what.
  - title: Long-term maintainability
    text: Standard components, documentation and automation, so the system outlives any one person or supplier.
