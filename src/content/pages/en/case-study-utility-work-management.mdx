- type: cards
  background: light
  anchorId: challenges
  eyebrow: The geospatial challenges
  heading: Six challenges of a work management system
  items:
  - tag: '1'
    title: Thousands of items, changing all day
    text: Work items are created, assigned, started and closed continuously. A map that reloads everything on every change is unusable. Updates must be cheap and visible quickly.
  - tag: '2'
    title: Work belongs to assets, not only addresses
    text: A job sits on a pole, a cable, a main or a meter, often described in several systems with different quality. Each item needs a reliable location and a link to the network.
  - tag: '3'
    title: Readable from region to street
    text: Thousands of points must stay legible in the overview and detailed at street level, with status and priority visible, without choking the browser.
  - tag: '4'
    title: Spatial questions, answered fast
    text: Which urgent gas jobs are open within a few kilometres of this crew, due today, needing a certain skill? Space, attributes and time must combine at interactive speed, for many users at once.
  - tag: '5'
    title: Travel time, not straight lines
    text: Real travel depends on one-way streets, restrictions, access and road type. Travel times between many crews and many work locations are needed again and again.
  - tag: '6'
    title: The best plan, recomputed quickly
    text: Assigning and sequencing jobs must respect skills, shifts and priorities, put emergency work first, and adapt when the day changes. It has to take seconds, not hours.

- type: cards
  background: light
  anchorId: solution
  eyebrow: The solution
  heading: Built on open source geospatial technology
  items:
  - tag: PostgreSQL + PostGIS
    title: One spatial source of truth
    text: Work items, assets and the road network live in one spatial database. Spatial indexes make questions like "what is open, urgent and near here" interactive.
    link: /technology#database
  - tag: Vector tiles
    title: A live map that stays light
    text: The map is served as vector tiles generated from PostGIS. Thousands of points draw smoothly, styling by status and priority happens in the browser, and changes reach the map shortly after they happen.
    link: /technology#services
  - tag: MapLibre GL JS
    title: The dispatcher's map
    text: A fast WebGL map with clustering in the overview, detail at street level, filters by status, type, crew and date, and selection tools for asking questions about an area.
    link: /technology#frontend
  - tag: pgRouting
    title: Routing on the real network
    text: Travel routes and times are computed on the road network inside the database, next to the data. Travel-time matrices between crews and work locations feed the optimisation.
    link: /technology#database
  - tag: Optimisation
    title: Assign and sequence the work
    text: From the open work, the crews and the travel times, the module proposes assignments and the order of visits, shown on the same map, and recomputes when the day's work changes.
