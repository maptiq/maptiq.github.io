## Short version

This website sets no cookies of its own and loads nothing from third parties when you open a page. Fonts are hosted on our own server. Google Analytics measures visits only if you accept it in the cookie banner, see the [cookie policy](/en/cookies/).

## Who is responsible

MaptIQ Consulting Kft., 1171 Budapest, Réti Csík utca 3., Hungary. Email: [info@maptiq.eu](mailto:info@maptiq.eu).

## Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, the host processes technical data such as your IP address and the requested page in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. We have no access to personal data in these logs.

## Analytics

If you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) to measure how the website is used. It sets cookies and sends usage data, with IP anonymisation, to Google, which may transfer it to the USA. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Without your consent nothing is loaded.

## Contact form and email

If you write to us by email or through the contact form, we use your name, email address and message only to answer you. The form is sent through Web3Forms, which forwards it to our mailbox, and uses hCaptcha for spam protection. hCaptcha is loaded only when you start filling in the form. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and for legal retention periods (check).

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its processing, and complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu).

## Changes

We update this policy when the website changes.
